Hybrid RAG over EU & Irish law

A retrieval pipeline over EU technology regulation and Irish immigration law, with an evaluation layer that measures retrieval accuracy, faithfulness and hallucination rate.

Live demo Source & eval report

Two incompatible question shapes

"AI Act Article 6(2)" and "Stamp 1G" need exact tokens, because embeddings blur an article number into its neighbours. "Can my spouse work on a Critical Skills permit" shares no vocabulary at all with the statute that answers it.

So both retrievers run, and Reciprocal Rank Fusion merges them using ranks rather than scores, which means two incompatible scales never have to be normalised against each other. BM25 comes from SQLite FTS5, which ships in the standard library with a built-in bm25() ranking function, so lexical search costs zero dependencies and zero RAM.

93.3%hybrid recall@10
91.7%BM25 alone
86.7%dense alone
80→100%multi-hop recall

Abstention is measured, not hoped for

Ten of the 68 evaluation questions have no answer in the corpus, built from real traps. Ask for the minimum salary threshold for an Irish EU Blue Card and there isn't one, because Ireland has a Title V opt-out and is not bound by the Blue Card Directive. A system that answers confidently is hallucinating, and the only way to know is to ask it. Every chunk carries an ireland_bound flag, and instruments that do not bind Ireland are labelled inline in the prompt context.

95.2% faithfulness, 4.8% hallucination, and 100% of citations resolving to a real source.

The evaluation deleted one of this project's own features

A retry gate fired whenever an answer's grounding score fell below 0.45, on the theory that a quality measurement should change the output rather than just be displayed. Measuring it killed it.

Across 36 evaluated answers grounding ran 0.68 to 0.96, so the retry never fired once. Worse, splitting those answers at the median, the lower-grounding half scored 98.2% faithfulness against 91.7% for the upper half. The signal is inversely related to the thing it was gating on: an answer that paraphrases a statute in plain English scores lower on similarity than one that copies its wording, while being just as accurate.

No threshold fixes that, so the retry is gone rather than retuned, and nothing replaced it, because there is no evidence yet that a replacement would help. What survives is the part that is measured: the multi-search loop, which takes multi-hop recall from 80% to 100%.
